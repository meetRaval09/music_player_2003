DEMO MUSIC FOR PIXEL TUNES 2003
================================

These 4 tracks are ORIGINAL 8-bit chiptune compositions made for this project.
They do not use any existing song, melody, or sample.

  01-pixel-sunrise.mp3
  02-arcade-night.mp3
  03-rainy-cartridge.mp3
  04-level-complete.mp3

License: CC0 1.0 (public domain). Free to use, copy, change and share, for any purpose,
with no credit needed.

How to try them:
  1. Open Pixel Tunes 2003.
  2. Click "+ FOLDER".
  3. Choose this "demo-music" folder.
  4. Press the orange play button.

Remember: the player makes its own copy of any song you add. To remove the demo songs later,
click the red X next to a song, or press CLEAR.
